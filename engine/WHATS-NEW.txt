# What the Wine modules' catalog brings since the one before it: a line for each
# thing, in words for whoever plays. The build puts these lines into modules.json as
# "notes"; the app shows them with the update and the agent in its notification.
# Rewrite them with every change to the modules: what stays here is said again with
# the next catalog. Lines that start with # are not carried.
CrossOver 26.3 has the controller improvements the Previews have: a DualSense over Bluetooth is shown to games as one on a cable, with its triggers and haptics; XInput games get rumble; a game that sets no trigger effect can have one; the DualShock 4 is covered.
These 26.3 modules were checked with a probe of what a game finds, and have not been played in a game yet.
