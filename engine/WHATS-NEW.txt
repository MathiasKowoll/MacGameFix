2XKO (Riot) now starts and plays on CrossOver Preview 41303, in a CrossOver bottle and through NotProton (Rosetta), signed in through the Riot Client. The patches that make it run are the work of NotProton's creator: thank you.
It stutters at times: its copy protection keeps the game's code encrypted, and Rosetta pays for every page it opens. How to set it up: docs/improvements/2xko.md.
After this update each bottle and prefix of that CrossOver refreshes its copies of Wine's modules the next time it starts, which takes a few seconds once.
